> **Thesis:** “Counting colors isn’t enough.” The precedent argues that any system surfacing people or culture-laden artifacts must balance **who is present** with **for whom the set ‘speaks’.** Presence alone can yield tokenistic diversity without user resonance.

**3.1 Claims**

- Diversity ≠ Inclusion – presence bounds do **not** guarantee personal relevance.
- Metrics as boundary objects – they make value choices explicit and negotiable.

**3.2 Tactics**

- Mathematical formalism → lends legitimacy to social critique.
- Interactive blocks demo → translates abstract scores into visceral “aha” moments for non-experts.

**3.3 Critical edge**

- Calls out industry habit of conflating fairness with surface heterogeneity (Mitchell M. et al. 2020).
- Invites stakeholders to choose aggregator that matches moral stance (egalitarian vs utilitarian).
